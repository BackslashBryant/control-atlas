# GovFrame Framework Mapper PRD

## Purpose

GovFrame reduces the manual work required to compare federal and DoD cybersecurity frameworks. A professional can begin with any supported item and see direct sourced mappings, explained calculated paths, unmapped status, and evidence quality.

## Primary Users

- Assessors and auditors comparing obligations.
- Security and compliance engineers translating requirements.
- System owners evaluating overlap and evidence reuse.
- Program teams building framework-to-framework matrices.

## Core Experiences

1. Search and investigate one framework item.
2. Build a whole-framework or selected-item mapping matrix.
3. Browse complete public catalogs and honest mapping coverage.
4. Audit the gold, silver, and bronze evidence behind a mapping.

## Scope

First-class registry entries cover NIST SP 800-53, NIST SP 800-171, NIST CSF, CMMC, FedRAMP, Control Correlation Identifiers, NIST AI RMF, NIST SSDF, and the DoD Responsible AI Toolkit. Catalogs and mappings publish only when supported by available public evidence.

Control Correlation Identifiers are granular bridge requirements. They may connect high-level controls to mappable technical requirements, but they are not synonymous with STIGs and do not make STIG catalog ingestion a product goal.

## Constraints

- Static GitHub Pages delivery.
- No accounts, telemetry, cookies, or user-data storage.
- Gold evidence decides canonical truth.
- Direct and calculated relationships remain visibly distinct.
- Absence of a sourced mapping is reported honestly.

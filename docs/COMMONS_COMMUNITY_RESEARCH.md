## What qualifies

Resources lists a destination only when a practitioner can reach a useful artifact or community without buying the publisher's product. A commercial publisher may be named because it publishes a clearly accessible template, documentation set, benchmark, or community surface. The publisher's consulting offer, managed service, subscription product, or sales funnel is not itself a Resource.

Access-restricted government systems are a different case. They may be listed when the official owner and operational purpose are verifiable and the card plainly says whether a CAC, authorized account, invitation, or DoD network is required.

Control Atlas does not claim a precise activity level, membership count, endorsement, or response time without durable primary evidence. Each community detail page repeats this warning exactly once:

> Do not post CUI, credentials, system details, assessment evidence, or other non-public organizational information.

## Commercial-publisher boundary

Accepted examples are the artifact, not the seller:

- I-Assure's publicly reachable RMF artifact templates
- CIS Benchmarks' no-cost PDF access and CIS WorkBench's free account path
- AWS GovCloud and Microsoft government-cloud public documentation
- Red Hat's public OpenSCAP documentation

Rejected examples include Tenable/Nessus paid-product documentation and audit-file downloads, Platform One managed services such as Party Bus, and gated vendor lead magnets. Their disposition and concrete rejection reasons remain in the candidate ledger. A resource can be reconsidered only when a stable, directly useful, no-cost public artifact is verified.

## Research discipline

- Prefer official owner pages and maintained project repositories.
- Do not infer purpose or access from a search snippet.
- Do not turn an ingested Library publication into a duplicate Resource.
- Do not mirror binaries, paid content, private workspaces, or unlicensed material.
- Record canonical URL, owner, access, cost boundary, maintenance state, verification date, and inclusion reason in the dataset.
- Preserve failures and restricted-access outcomes honestly in the health report.

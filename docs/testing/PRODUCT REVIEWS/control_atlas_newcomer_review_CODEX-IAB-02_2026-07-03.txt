1. FIRST CONTACT

- Sam thought this was a public reference map for federal cyber compliance. The landing page said it could help explore controls, baselines, STIGs, templates, sources, and playbooks without login or upload.

- Sam was still intimidated by the first-screen terms: controls, baselines, STIGs, RMF/ATO templates, sources, and mappings. The page offered paths, but the words already assumed some compliance vocabulary.

----------------------------------------------------------------
2. ON-RAMP VERDICT (BLUF)
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Can a true newcomer become productive using ONLY this app,
  no course/mentor/external docs? PARTIAL.
- Control Atlas can get Sam oriented, teach many terms, trace a STIG-to-CCI-to-NIST chain, explain inheritance, and generate a blank worksheet. That is real newcomer value.
- The weak point is not navigation; it is translation at the moment of action. Control records and relationship tables still rely too much on official wording and acronyms, so Sam can follow the app but not always understand what to do next.
- A newcomer can complete starter tasks inside the app, but would still need a mentor or outside training before trusting their own interpretation of an RMF package.
